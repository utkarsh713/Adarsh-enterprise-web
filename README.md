## 📌 About The Project

**Adarsh Enterprise** is a modern and responsive business website created to showcase a wide range of festival, decoration, lighting, gifting, and party products.

The website provides customers with an easy way to:

- 🎉 Explore festival collections
- 🛍️ Browse popular product categories
- 📍 Find the business location
- 📞 Contact the business directly
- 💬 Connect through WhatsApp
- 📧 Send enquiries through the contact section

The website is designed with a clean, attractive, and festive UI to provide a smooth experience on both desktop and mobile devices.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Hero Section
- Attractive landing section
- Animated typing text
- Festival-themed visual elements
- Quick navigation buttons
- Business statistics

### 🎆 Festival Collections

The website includes dedicated collections for different celebrations:

- 🪔 Diwali
- 🎨 Holi
- 🇮🇳 Independence Day
- 🇮🇳 Republic Day
- 🦚 Janmashtami
- 🌙 Eid
- 🎡 Local Mela & Events

Each festival section contains relevant products and an enquiry option.

### 🛍️ Product Categories

Popular product categories include:

- 🪔 Diyas & Candles
- 💡 LED & Fairy Lights
- 🎁 Gift Items
- 🎊 Party Decorations
- 🏳️ Flags & Banners
- 🎀 Torans & Decorative Items
- 🎉 Festival Accessories

### 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

- 💻 Desktop
- 💻 Laptop
- 📱 Mobile
- 📟 Tablet

### ✨ Animations

The website uses smooth animations and scroll effects to make the interface more interactive and engaging.

### 📞 Contact Section

Customers can directly access:

- 📱 Phone
- 💬 WhatsApp
- 📧 Email
- 📍 Location
- 🕐 Working Hours

Users can submit their requirements by selecting a festival/event and entering their message.
